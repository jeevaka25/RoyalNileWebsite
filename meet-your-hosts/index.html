Abdol: your host in Luxor

A Luxor native with a passion for Egyptology, Abdol is an Airbnb Superhost and co-owner of Royal Nile Villas. His approach is personal: help guests feel welcome, understand their plans and experience the hospitality of his home city. You may see his name written as “Abdul” on his Airbnb profile.

Abdol and fellow co-owner Jeevaka manage guest contact directly. Our on-site hospitality and restaurant team looks after the property and meals, while the owners remain involved in planning and communication. We do not hand the guest relationship over to an unrelated management company.

Read the reviews for yourself

On 9 September 2026, Abdol’s public Airbnb profile showed 1,940 reviews, a 4.91 out of 5 average rating and Superhost status. These are host-profile figures across his listings, not the review count of any single apartment. Ratings and totals can change.

The people behind your stay

  • Owners: direct guest contact, arrangements and agency coordination.
  • Property and restaurant team: on-site accommodation service, housekeeping and meals.
  • Tour guides: a network of vetted, licensed Egyptology guides in a range of languages. Your guide language and availability are confirmed for your booking.
  • Drivers and vehicles: private air-conditioned transport arranged to fit your party, luggage and itinerary.

Our broad guide and vehicle network supports tours year-round. Accommodation inventory, guide language and vehicle availability are separate checks. We confirm the details rather than promising a particular person before availability is agreed.

One local team, two complementary websites

Egypt Villa Tours presents our accommodation, dining and Luxor experiences. Egyptian Tours is our sister website for private tours and travel planning. Both belong to the same owners; a recommendation between them is a recommendation from our own business.

Royal Nile Villas and Royal Nile Suites share a location. Royal Home is a separate property in our collection. Each booking proposal identifies the actual accommodation and rooming arrangement.

Practical information, with clear sources

Our travel guides combine our stated operating arrangements with linked official visitor information. Opening hours, ticket categories and access rules should be checked again close to travel. Planning suggestions are not guarantees of journey time or entry.

Archaeological claims are linked to their sources. We identify a named contributor or reviewer only when they have actually contributed and agreed to be credited. If you spot an error or a changed local arrangement, contact the owners so we can check it and update the relevant page.
