Choose your bank around your days

Luxor’s West Bank suits travellers who want a quieter base, apartment space and mornings among the tombs and temples of ancient Thebes. The East Bank suits visitors who prioritise central restaurants, large hotels and walking to Luxor Temple. Neither is the right answer for everyone: compare the actual entrance of your accommodation with the places you want to visit.

This planner is published by Egypt Villa Tours, whose owners operate West Bank accommodation. It includes alternatives to our own properties so you can choose according to your needs.

Three West Bank settings to compare

Near the passenger ferry: useful when you want frequent crossings into central Luxor. Ask for the actual walking route, including luggage handling, steps and the return journey after dinner. A river view does not automatically mean easy access to the landing.

Village and riverside accommodation: often chosen for gardens, views and a calmer setting. Confirm road access, nearby dining, evening transport and whether a shuttle has fixed hours. Royal Nile Villas is in Al Aqaletah and provides a free shuttle to the ferry landing daily from 9am to 9pm. Arrange your departure with the team and ask about transport for any return after 9pm.

Near the Theban monuments: useful for travellers focused on tombs and temples, but check how you will reach the river and restaurants. “Near the Valley of the Kings” is not a complete transport plan.

A simple map of the decisions

A short boat crossing is not a door-to-door journey

Build each crossing from five parts: accommodation to landing, waiting and boarding, the river crossing, disembarking, then onward transport or walking. For a car transfer, ask whether you will travel by road via the bridge. The quickest-looking line on a map may not match the route a vehicle can take.

Ask for separate estimates for a normal sightseeing departure, a late return and an airport transfer. Tell the driver your flight time, terminal information if supplied, luggage count and any child-seat needs. Do not use a sightseeing transfer estimate as your airport check-in buffer.

Rooming: count beds and bathrooms, not just guests

For families or groups, make one row per apartment. Record the number of adults and children, exact beds, bathrooms, floor, steps, balcony access and whether any beds are in shared living space. Ask about cots, additional beds and accessible routes individually; a ground-floor label alone does not prove step-free access.

Download the blank group rooming checklist (CSV). It contains no guest information. Use initials rather than unnecessary personal details when sharing an initial enquiry.

  • Confirm whether the pool and outdoor spaces are shared.
  • Check kitchen equipment, meal arrangements and dietary requirements.
  • Ask about stair access before selecting an upper-floor penthouse.
  • Agree housekeeping, laundry and late check-in expectations.
  • For children, ask about pool supervision arrangements and balcony safety.

Before you commit

Request a written proposal showing the property and apartment names, bed layout, dates, total price and currency, included meals and transfers, deposit, cancellation terms, hold expiry and your direct contact. For the eclipse period, ask for the specific viewing arrangements and any optional sightseeing costs. Keep room availability and tour availability as separate confirmations.
