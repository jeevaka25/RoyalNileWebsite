Luxor West Bank Accommodation With a Pool and Kitchen: What to Check Before Booking

“Pool and kitchen” sounds precise, but listings use both terms loosely. This checklist helps you verify what you are actually booking.

Published 2026-08-07 · Updated 2026-08-07

Start with the pool definition

A property can advertise a pool that is private to one villa, shared between several apartments, shared with outside visitors or only available seasonally. Ask who uses it, the hours, depth, heating status, children’s rules and whether the photographed pool belongs to the exact building. Royal Nile Villas has a shared 14-metre pool within a 30-metre garden. Some ground-floor units have direct pool access, while upper floors overlook it.

Inspect the kitchen photograph

The word “kitchen” may describe anything from a kettle and hotplate to a full room with refrigerator, hob and preparation space. Look for the actual unit’s photographs and ask about cookware, knives, plates, drinking-water arrangements and nearby groceries. Royal Nile Villas apartments include kitchen facilities, but guests with a critical equipment need should confirm it for the exact apartment before arrival.

Match the layout to the people

The combination of pool and kitchen is especially attractive to families and longer-stay guests, but the bedroom and bathroom layout still decides comfort. Royal Nile Villas units generally have two bedrooms and sleep up to four. Bed combinations include two queens or one queen with two singles, and bathroom counts vary from one to two. Never reserve only from the shared amenity list.

Ask what is shared beyond the pool

Gardens, entrances, rooftops and restaurants may also be common spaces. Shared does not mean inferior; it simply needs to be understood. A small apartment property often feels calmer than a large resort, but guests should know whether events, restaurant visitors or other units use the same areas. If privacy is the priority, choose an upper-floor private terrace or a unit positioned away from common circulation.

Check shade, cleanliness and maintenance

Pool photographs rarely show the practical details. Ask about shade, loungers, towels, cleaning and how maintenance closures are handled. For the kitchen, ask who to contact if an appliance fails and how housekeeping works during a longer stay. Recent guest reviews are more informative than an old amenity badge. A well-managed shared pool and working kitchen are more valuable than a theoretically private feature with poor upkeep.

Do not ignore location and transport

A beautiful pool is less useful if every meal or tour requires a complicated journey. Confirm airport arrival, ferry access, tour pickup and food options. Royal Nile Villas provides an on-site Nile-view restaurant, free ferry shuttle and optional airport and tour transport. That combination lets guests use the kitchen selectively instead of depending on it for every meal.

The booking checklist

Before paying, request the exact apartment name, floor, bedroom and bathroom count, bed configuration, kitchen equipment, pool-sharing rules, view, cancellation terms and total price. Save the answer with the reservation. If an amenity is essential because of a child, disability, allergy or long stay, say so explicitly. Clear requirements give the host a chance to confirm the right unit or tell you honestly when another property would be better.
