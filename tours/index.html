Before you book

Less guesswork. More time exploring.

01

Check what is included

Private does not always mean a guide is included. Our West and East Bank tours include an Egyptologist; several longer trips offer a guide as an extra. Check entrance tickets and other exclusions on each tour page.

02

Leave room in your day

A flight duration is not the full outing time. Airport, hotel and boat transfers add time, and Abu Simbel involves a particularly long drive. Confirm the pickup location and schedule for your dates.

03

Keep your stay connected

Choose a West Bank apartment as your base, then ask us to coordinate your experiences and transfers. Share your dates and priorities so we can suggest a workable sequence.
